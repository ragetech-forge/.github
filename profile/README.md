### The promise

- **No telemetry.** The apps never phone home.
- **No accounts.** Install it and use it.
- **No Electron.** Native Rust, small and quick to start.
- **Your files, your rules.** Nothing is moved or deleted without you knowing.

### Get involved

- Found a bug? Open an issue in the app's repo.
- Have an idea for a small Linux tool? [Start a discussion](https://github.com/orgs/ragetech-forge/discussions) or open an issue.
- Want to help? Look for issues labeled `good first issue`.
